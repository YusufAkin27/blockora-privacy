Kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar

Android ayarlarından (Ayarlar → Google → Reklamlar) reklam kimliğini sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsin.

Veri silme

Sıralamadaki adının ve skorlarının silinmesini istersen, Oyun'daki adını ve yaklaşık skorunu belirterek ysufakin23@gmail.com adresine yaz; 30 gün içinde silinir. Oyunu kaldırmak cihazdaki tüm verileri siler.

Çocuklar

Oyun 13 yaş altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir ve bilerek 13 yaş altından veri toplamaz.

English

Blockora: Block Puzzle (the "Game") is developed by Akin Studios. This policy explains what data the Game collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

DataPurposeStored at
Player name (the nickname you type)Shown on the global leaderboardLeaderboard server (Neon, USA)
Random player ID (a UUID created on your device, contains no personal information)Linking your scores to youYour device and the leaderboard server
Your best scoresLeaderboardLeaderboard server
Advertising ID, device and usage informationServing and measuring ads, fraud preventionUnity LevelPlay and its ad partners

The Game has no accounts and does not collect email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. Settings (sound, language, etc.) stay on your device.

Third parties

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and ad networks – advertising. Privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) – hosting of leaderboard data. https://neon.tech/privacy-policy

Other players only see your name and score; your player ID is never shared.

Personalised ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Data deletion

To have your leaderboard name and scores deleted, email ysufakin23@gmail.com with your in-game name and approximate score; it will be deleted within 30 days. Uninstalling the Game removes all data from your device.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be published on this page.
